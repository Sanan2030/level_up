## Why it matters

The planned lesson uses a concrete delivery decision to show when **Requirements and design options** helps a team. It will make assumptions and affected stakeholders explicit.

## Core theory: planned outline

1. Define the essential terms in the topic.
2. Assess consistency, completeness, value and risk across interdependent requirements.
3. Compare a useful application with a plausible misuse.
4. Identify evidence that could change the decision.

## Common mistakes and best practices

- Do not mistake a template or framework for evidence.
- State the context and decision owner before recommending an action.
- Compare outcomes rather than counting documents or features.
