## Concept

A Scrum Team has one Product Owner, one Scrum Master and Developers, with no sub-teams or internal hierarchies. The whole team is accountable for a valuable, useful Increment each Sprint. The Product Owner is accountable for maximizing product value and effective Product Backlog management; this does not require personally writing every item.

## Why it matters

The Product Owner develops the Product Goal and ensures items are understood and ordered. Work can be delegated, while accountability remains. Developers own sizing, selection of Sprint work and the implementation plan. The Scrum Master establishes Scrum and enables team effectiveness rather than allocating technical tasks.

## Decision principles

Chapter 1 of the book explains delays when Product Owners act merely as scribes or proxies. Product decisions need authority and access to customers. These types are the authors’ explanatory model, not extra Scrum accountabilities. The book’s older Development Team terminology is updated here to Developers from the 2020 Guide.

## Worked scenario

In a booking product, an analyst writes backlog items. Sales wants the Product Owner to allocate developers’ daily tasks. Developers propose a smaller technical solution.

## Decision reasoning

The analyst may keep drafting; the Product Owner retains accountability for direction, ordering and transparency. Agree goals and quality boundaries with Developers and let them plan implementation. Hear sales’ needs without turning Product Ownership into a committee.

## Common pitfalls

Making the Product Owner the only channel to users; treating the Scrum Master as a project manager; confusing delegation with transferred accountability.

## Application exercise

Allocate three decisions: item ordering, technical solution, and improving team collaboration. Name collaborators for each.

## Explained solution

<summary>Show explained solution</summary>

The Product Owner is accountable for ordering, Developers own technical planning, and the Scrum Master helps improve effectiveness. Relevant experts collaborate without erasing decision ownership.
