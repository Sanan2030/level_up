## Concept

EBM 2024 describes strategic goals as ambitious and uncertain, intermediate goals as achievements indicating progress, and immediate tactical goals as the current improvement focus. No annual, quarterly or weekly cadence is mandated. Scrum’s Product Goal gives one longer-term product focus; the EBM levels are not three mandatory Scrum artifacts.

## Why it matters

The book’s Vision and Validation chapters start with the audience, problem and value proposition. A roadmap can expose intended outcomes and assumptions rather than binding every solution permanently. An MVP approach can test a critical assumption with limited investment; every planned feature need not be built. Internal approval alone does not validate market benefit.

## Decision principles

Before experimenting, record the baseline, audience, expected change, observation window and guardrail. Inspect results and adapt the goal or approach. Do not retrospectively redefine success to fit any result. Small samples and concurrent changes weaken causal claims; acknowledge evidence quality. These experiment-design cautions are editorial teaching guidance, not official exam rules.

### The business model is also a hypothesis

Chapter 2 examines customer segments, value propositions, channels, relationships, revenue and costs. These are product hypotheses rather than immutable truths outside the Product Backlog. If acquisition is costly and retention is weak, shipping more features may not solve the economic problem. Distinguish outcomes experienced by users from impacts achieved by the organization, then inspect the proposed connection. A Business Model Canvas is one possible technique, not a prescribed Scrum artifact.

## Worked scenario

A bank wants more self-service bookings. The team proposes six months of AI development, but does not know where users struggle.

## Decision reasoning

Observe the booking journey first. As a teaching hypothesis, test whether completion for a comparable segment can rise from 40% to 50% in two weeks, with no increase in support complaints. These are scenario thresholds, not prescribed standards. AI is not assumed to be the proven solution.

## Common pitfalls

Reducing strategy to a feature list; choosing success criteria after an experiment; continuing a weak direction because of sunk costs.

## Application exercise

Write one strategic outcome, one intermediate goal and one tactical experiment. What observation would change your mind?

## Explained solution

<details>
<summary>Show explained solution</summary>

Strategic: more customers book independently. Intermediate: completion improves for a chosen segment. Tactical: simplify slot selection. If completion is unchanged and complaints increase, revert and revisit the problem.

## Check understanding and remember

Goal → hypothesis → small experiment → measurement → adaptation. Update the roadmap as evidence changes.
